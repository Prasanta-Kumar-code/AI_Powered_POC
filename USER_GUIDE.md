# User Guide

## Create and Download a Resume

1. Open the resume generator in a modern browser. No account is required.
2. Enter your contact details, work history, education, skills, and notable achievements. Add only accurate information.
3. Choose the job title or target role. Review the AI provider notice before submitting; the details needed for generation are sent to that provider.
4. Select **Generate** and wait for the draft. If generation fails or a free-tier limit is reached, keep your entered details and try again later.
5. Read the complete draft carefully. Correct errors, remove anything inaccurate, and tailor it to the job. AI output is a draft, not a verification of your experience.
6. Use the plain-text version when an application requests text or an ATS-friendly format.
7. Select **Download PDF** to save the reviewed resume to your device. Open the PDF and check page breaks, contact details, and formatting before sending it.
8. Clear the form or close the page when finished, especially on a shared device.

The app does not require a database or account. The AI provider may process submitted information under its own policies, so avoid entering sensitive details that are not needed for a resume.
