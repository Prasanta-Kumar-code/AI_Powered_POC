# Business Model

## Customer Segments
The initial segment is knowledge-intensive teams with recurring policy, support, operations, or product questions and enough internal content to benefit from grounded search. Prioritize organizations that value auditability and have an identifiable workspace administrator.

## Value Proposition
AtlasAI reduces time spent locating and validating internal information while lowering the risk of unsupported AI answers. The differentiated value is permission-aware evidence, operational visibility, and a path from answers to governed workflows.

## Packaging
- **Pilot:** limited seats, sources, storage, monthly query and token budget, guided onboarding.
- **Team:** per-seat base price plus included usage, standard connectors, source versioning, and workspace administration.
- **Business:** higher limits, SSO, retention controls, audit export, priority support, and advanced evaluation reporting.
- **Enterprise:** negotiated commitment, private networking options, dedicated support, custom connectors, and contractual security requirements.

Usage dimensions should remain understandable: active seats, indexed storage, queries, and model usage. Avoid pricing solely on raw tokens because customers cannot predict the value or cost easily.

## Unit Economics
Track acquisition cost, activation, weekly retained workspaces, cost per answered query, ingestion cost per GB, support cost, gross margin, and expansion revenue. Set workspace budgets and provider controls so gross margin cannot be destroyed by abusive or unexpectedly long queries.

## Go-to-Market
Start with design partners whose document corpus and question patterns can produce labeled evaluation data. Measure time-to-first-value, verified-answer rate, source coverage, repeat usage, and administrator effort. Sell trust and measurable workflow improvement, not generic access to a chatbot.

## Governance and Trust
Publish data handling, retention, model provider, and security commitments. Give customers control over sources, deletion, access, and model-use settings. A clear abstention and citation experience supports adoption more sustainably than exaggerated accuracy claims.

## Expansion Paths
Expand from Q&A to source comparison, recurring briefings, approved integrations, and human-reviewed action workflows. Each expansion must preserve the same authorization, audit, and evaluation standards.

## Common Mistakes
- Charging for activity that customers cannot forecast.
- Selling autonomous action before approval and rollback controls exist.
- Measuring signups instead of verified outcomes and retained workspaces.
- Ignoring ingestion and support costs in gross-margin planning.
- Making enterprise promises that the operational model cannot support.
